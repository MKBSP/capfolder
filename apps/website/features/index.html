What Capfolder does

One page per feature: what it does, the checks it runs for you, where your data goes, and what it doesn't do yet. Each links to a step-by-step guide with screenshots.

  • Works with the AI you already use

    Run the cap table from Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Codex or Gemini CLI. Every change is previewed first and nothing is saved until you say yes.

  • Import from Pulley or Carta

    Bring your cap table over from Pulley, Carta, Cake, an OCF file or a spreadsheet. Every total is checked against the old tool before it's saved.

  • Leaving Pulley before December 8

    The shutdown dates, what to download from Pulley, and how to move your cap table and documents with nothing lost.
