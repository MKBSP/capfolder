What your AI can do today

  • Read everything. The cap table as of any date, each person's vesting, what's missing before diligence, and the text of any filed document.
  • Set up the company. Founders and their vesting, the option pool, SAFEs from the YC template, a 409A. Each one previewed, then recorded on your yes.
  • Not incorporated yet? Record the shares you promise each cofounder. When you incorporate, one step turns them into real shares.
  • File documents. Point it at a signed PDF; it says what the document proves and files it against the right line.
  • Share the folder with the app. The desktop app and your AI write the same folder, and a lock keeps them from overwriting each other.

Add Capfolder to your AI

Pick yours. Then say "Set up my cap table". Needs Node 22 or newer, which Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI already use.

  • Claude Code

    /plugin marketplace add MKBSP/capfolder
    /plugin install capfolder@capfolder

  • Claude Desktop

    Download capfolder.mcpb from GitHub releases and double-click it. Claude asks once to add Capfolder.

  • Codex

    codex mcp add capfolder -- npx -y capfolder mcp

  • Gemini CLI

    gemini extensions install https://github.com/MKBSP/capfolder

  • One command for whichever you have

    npx capfolder install

    Finds Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI on your computer and sets each one up. The same setup is in the app under Settings → AI → Connect your AI.

Rules your AI can't break

  • Preview, then yes. A change is written only after you confirm the exact preview you were shown. A preview expires in 10 minutes, and it is refused if the folder changed in between.
  • The engine does the math. Your AI never adds up shares itself; it reads every number from Capfolder.
  • Open Cap Table Format. Every record is checked against the OCF schema before it is written, and every write is logged in events.jsonl.
  • Documents are data. Text inside a PDF can't trigger anything. If a document contains instructions, your AI tells you instead of following them.
  • No legal advice. Ask "should I", and it points you to your lawyer and shows what the cap table says.

Questions people ask

Which AI can I use?

Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Codex and Gemini CLI today. ChatGPT is coming. Inside the desktop app, Ask AI works with Ollama, LM Studio or your own Claude, OpenAI or Gemini key.

Can the AI mess up my cap table?

No. Every change comes back as a preview first: the exact records and the cap table after. Nothing is saved until you say yes, and every saved change is checked against the Open Cap Table Format and logged.

Does my data go to an AI company?

Only what your AI reads to answer you, and only if you use a cloud AI. The folder stays on your computer. Use a local model and nothing leaves it.

Is this legal advice?

No. Capfolder records what you and your lawyer decided and fills in YC's standard SAFE word for word. Have a lawyer review your documents.
