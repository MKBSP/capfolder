A Pulley alternative you own

Pulley shuts down on December 8, 2026. Its offer is a move to Carta on a 12-month contract. Capfolder is the other way out: a free, open-source app that keeps your cap table in a folder on your computer. No setup, and no company that can switch it off.

In short: if you used Pulley for SAFEs, option grants, vesting and signatures, Capfolder covers it for free. If you need 409A valuations, employee logins or round modelling today, Capfolder doesn't have them yet (see the roadmap below).

This comparison is written by the Capfolder team. Checked October 2026.

What happens to Pulley

  • The app closes on December 8, 2026. You can still reach your data in a limited way until January 31, 2027.
  • Carta is Pulley's exclusive migration partner. You opt in by November 30, 2026 and sign a 12-month Carta agreement. Carta matches your first-year Pulley price.
  • If you don't pick a provider, you lose access to your cap table, and Pulley won't help you move anywhere else. Download everything before December 8.

Sources: Pulley's shutdown FAQ, the banner on pulley.com/pricing, Eqvista's summary (announced September 15, 2026). Checked October 2026.

Pros and cons

Pulley did well

  • Easy to use, with fast, friendly support (G2 reviews).
  • One-click option grants from templates.
  • 409A, round modelling and employee logins in one place.
  • Cheaper than Carta.

Where Pulley falls short

  • It's shutting down. The only offered path is a 12-month Carta contract.
  • No OCF export, so moving your data is harder.
  • Onboarding could be easier, and modelling can't be exported (Capterra reviews).

Capfolder does well

  • Free, with no setup. Download, open, done.
  • Your cap table is plain files you own. It can't be shut down.
  • Drop in your old Pulley documents and they're sorted; missing ones are flagged.
  • Every number shows its math.

Where Capfolder falls short

  • No 409A valuations, employee logins or round modelling yet.
  • One computer at a time until sync ships in Pro.
  • New: the installers aren't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so your computer warns you once.

Moving from Pulley, step by step

  1. Download your cap table: in Pulley, Dashboard → Download Cap Table. Choose the Detailed view and All time. (Pulley's guide)
  2. Download your documents: Company → Data Room → Download All. (Pulley's guide)
  3. Do both before December 8, 2026.
  4. In Capfolder, create your company folder, choose Import from Pulley or Carta, and pick the Excel file. You check the columns before anything is saved.
  5. Drop the documents folder in. Capfolder sorts them and lists what's missing. (Starting from zero instead?)
