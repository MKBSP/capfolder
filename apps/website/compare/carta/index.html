A free Carta alternative for early-stage founders

Carta does almost everything, and doesn't publish its prices. Capfolder covers what an early company uses (SAFEs, option grants, vesting, signatures) for free, in a folder you own.

In short: pre-seed or seed, mostly SAFEs and grants? Capfolder. Past Series A, with 409A and exit modelling to run? Carta.

Which one fits you

Choose Carta if…

  • You need 409A valuations and employee logins.
  • You run exit waterfalls and priced-round models.
  • Your investors expect a registered transfer agent.

Choose Capfolder if…

  • You want it free, with no setup.
  • You want your cap table in files you own.
  • You mostly issue SAFEs and option grants.

Moving from Carta in 3 steps

  1. Export from Carta

    Run reports: Cap table (Detailed), Securities ledger, Vesting, Stakeholder details. Documents library → download as zip.

  2. Import

    In Capfolder, choose Import from Pulley or Carta and pick the Excel file.

  3. Drop your documents

    Drop the folder in. Capfolder sorts it and lists what's missing.
