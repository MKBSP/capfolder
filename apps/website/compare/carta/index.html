A free Carta alternative for early-stage founders

Carta does almost everything, and charges for it: it doesn't publish its prices, and buyers report paying thousands a year. Capfolder covers what an early company actually uses (SAFEs, option grants, vesting, signatures) for free, with your cap table in a folder you own.

In short: past your Series A, with 409A valuations, employee logins and exit modelling to run? Carta is ahead. Pre-seed or seed, mostly SAFEs and a few grants? Capfolder does that for free, with no setup.

Pros and cons

Carta does well

  • The most complete tool: 409A, employee logins, round and exit modelling, an API.
  • Easy to use for day-to-day equity work (G2: 4.3/5 from 232 reviews).
  • A registered transfer agent, which some investors and lawyers expect.
  • Free for very small companies (up to 25 stakeholders and $1M raised).

Where Carta falls short

  • Prices aren't published, and reviewers say it gets expensive as teams grow (G2).
  • Reviewers mention onboarding problems, slow support and hard cancellation (Trustpilot).
  • Your data sits on Carta's servers. In 2024 Carta paused sales outreach after a customer said it used cap-table data to approach his investor (Fortune).
  • No OCF export: OCX is an Excel format.

Capfolder does well

  • Free, with no setup. Download, open, done.
  • Your cap table is plain files you own, on your computer.
  • Drop in your old documents and they're sorted; missing ones are flagged.
  • Every number shows its math, and the code is public.

Where Capfolder falls short

  • No 409A valuations, employee logins, round or exit modelling yet.
  • One computer at a time until sync ships in Pro.
  • Not a transfer agent, and new: the installers aren't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet.

Moving from Carta, step by step

  1. In Carta, go to Essentials → Run reports (or Cap Table → Run reports).
  2. Export the Cap table report (Detailed), the Securities ledger, the Vesting report and Stakeholder details to Excel. The OCX report is under Capitalization.
  3. Reports don't include documents. In the Documents library, select your files and download them as a zip (Carta's note): signed SAFEs, grants, board consents, 409As, plan documents.
  4. In Capfolder, create your company folder, choose Import from Pulley or Carta, and pick the Excel file. You check the columns before anything is saved.
  5. Drop the documents folder in. Capfolder sorts them and lists what's missing.
  6. Run both side by side for a few weeks before you cancel Carta.
