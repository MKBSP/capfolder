Why we built this

We lost our cap table paperwork.

We raised our seed on SAFEs from 28 investors. Some signed in Pulley. Some sent their own paperwork. Some signed by email.

When we needed the documents, the data was gone. We're now digging through old inboxes and asking investors to sign again.

A cap table is the most important record a company has. It shouldn't depend on a vendor, a subscription or a login. So we built one that lives in a folder you own, and open-sourced it so it can't disappear.

Questions

Who can see my data?

Your cap table and documents stay on your computer. A document you send for signature sits on the relay until it's signed, and whoever runs the relay could read it (self-host it if that matters). The app sends an anonymous install count; turn it off in Settings. Details below.

Can I move from Pulley or Carta?

Import their export (spreadsheet or OCF). Totals are checked against it before anything is saved. Then drop in your signed documents and they're sorted for you.

What does it cost?

The app is free. Pay what you want if it helps you. A paid hosted relay for teams may come later.

Which companies does it support?

Delaware C-corps first. More jurisdictions after.

Is this legal advice?

No. Capfolder records what you and your lawyer decided; it doesn't write your documents. Have a lawyer review them.

Privacy

This page. Fonts and images come from this site. Visits are counted with Plausible: no cookies, no personal data, your IP isn't stored. The star count and download links are read from GitHub's public API when the page loads, so GitHub sees that request.

The download form. When you click "Get the download", the form is loaded from Tally. It asks for your email and your computer, only to send you new versions. Ask through a GitHub issue to be removed, or reply to any email.

The app. Your cap table and documents stay in your folder. Documents you send for signature pass through the signing relay until everyone has signed, then the relay deletes them (within 7 days, or 30 if never signed). The app sends an anonymous install ping (a random install id, the app version, your operating system, and yes/no for "imported", "has documents", "sent for signature") once on first run and then weekly, only after you accept the terms. The relay keeps the day, not the time or your IP address. Turn it off in Settings → Privacy. The code that sends and stores it is public in the repo.
