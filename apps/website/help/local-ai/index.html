Connect Ollama or LM Studio

Capfolder's AI can run on a free model on your own computer. There's no bill, and nothing leaves your laptop.

Don't have Ollama or LM Studio yet? Start with Install a local AI. It takes about 10 minutes. Then come back here.

1. Start your local AI

  • Ollama: open the Ollama app. It keeps running in the background.
  • LM Studio: open LM Studio, go to the Developer tab and switch the server on, so it says Running.

You need at least one model downloaded. Not sure? See Install a local AI.

2. Open Settings → AI in Capfolder

Capfolder looks for Ollama and LM Studio on this computer when you open Settings. Under AI in use, each one shows what it found:

"3 models on this computer."
Ready. Go to step 3.
"running, but no model is downloaded yet."
Download a model first, then click Look again.
"Not running."
Start the app (step 1), then click Look again.

3. Pick your model

  1. In AI in use, choose your model. Local models end in (this computer), for example Ollama · llama3.1 (this computer).
  2. Tick Use AI when sorting documents if you want it to read and sort the PDFs you drop in.
  3. Open ✦ Ask AI and ask "What needs my attention?".
The AI only proposes. Every change it drafts opens for you to check, and nothing is saved until you click yes.

Not working?

Capfolder still says "Not running"
Capfolder looks on the usual ports: 11434 for Ollama and 1234 for LM Studio. If you changed the port, change it back.
Answers are slow
Pick a smaller model. A 3–8B model is enough for Capfolder.
I'd rather use Claude, OpenAI or Gemini
Pick it in AI in use and paste your own API key. It's kept in your computer's keychain. A cloud AI sees cap-table numbers and names, and reads documents only if you turn on Let cloud AI read documents.

Something unclear? Tell us on GitHub. Capfolder isn't legal advice: check your documents with a lawyer.
