Connect Ollama or LM Studio

Capfolder can use a free AI that runs on your own computer. You don't pay for it, and your cap table never leaves your laptop. This takes about 5 minutes.

Don't have Ollama or LM Studio yet? Do Install a local AI first, then come back here.

Orange boxes mark what to look at or click. Click a screenshot to enlarge it.

1. Make sure your local AI is running

  • Ollama: open the Ollama app. It runs quietly in the background (look for the llama icon in your Mac's menu bar or the Windows system tray).
  • LM Studio: open LM Studio, go to the Developer tab and switch the server on, so it says Running. (LM Studio's guide)

2. Open Settings in Capfolder

Click Settings at the top. Or, in your Get started list, click Set up AI. Then scroll to the AI box.

3. Check what Capfolder found

Capfolder looks for Ollama and LM Studio on your computer by itself. Under AI in use, it tells you what it found.

4. Pick your model

When Capfolder says models on this computer, open the AI in use menu and pick one. Models on your computer end in (this computer).

5. Try it

Click ✦ Ask AI at the top (or press ⌘K on a Mac, Ctrl+K on Windows) and type: What needs my attention?

The AI only suggests. Anything it drafts opens for you to check, and nothing is saved until you click yes.

Not working?

It still says "Not running"
Close and reopen Ollama or LM Studio, then click Look again. Capfolder looks in the usual place (port 11434 for Ollama, 1234 for LM Studio). If you changed that, change it back.
Answers are slow
Pick a smaller model. A small one (around 2–8B) is plenty for Capfolder.
I'd rather use Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini
Pick it in AI in use and paste your own API key. It's kept in your computer's keychain. A cloud AI sees cap-table numbers and names, and reads your documents only if you turn on Let cloud AI read documents.

Words used here

Local AI
An app that runs an AI on your own computer, instead of on a company's servers.
Model
The AI's "brain": a big file you download once. Smaller models are faster; bigger ones are smarter.
