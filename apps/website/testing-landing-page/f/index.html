Questions

We haven't incorporated or raised yet. Is it for us?

Yes. Record the split you agreed with your cofounders now. When you incorporate, it becomes real shares. When you raise, the SAFE is one form away.

Is it really free?

Yes, the whole app, with 5 signature requests a month. Pro, when it ships, adds sync and backup.

Who can see my data?

Your cap table and documents stay on your computer. A document you send for signature sits on the relay until it's signed (self-host the relay if that matters). The app sends an anonymous install count; turn it off in Settings.

Do I need to pay for AI?

No. Use a free model on your computer (Ollama or LM Studio). Your own Claude, OpenAI or Gemini key works too.

Which companies does it support?

Delaware C-corps first. More jurisdictions after.

Is this legal advice?

No. Capfolder records what you and your lawyer decided. It fills in YC's standard SAFE word for word and drafts nothing else. Have a lawyer review it.
