Questions

Will Capfolder find me investors?

No. Finding them is on you, and the guides help you know what to ask for. Capfolder does the paperwork once someone says yes.

What does "bring your own AI" mean?

Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or a model on your computer (Ollama or LM Studio). It answers from your folder and drafts changes. Every company folder also has an AGENTS.md, so Claude Code can work in it. Nothing is saved without your click.

Who can see my data?

Your cap table and documents stay on your computer. A document you send for signature waits on the relay until it's signed, then the relay deletes it. Self-host the relay if that matters to you.

Is this legal advice?

No. Capfolder fills in YC's standard SAFE word for word and drafts nothing else. Have a lawyer review your documents.
