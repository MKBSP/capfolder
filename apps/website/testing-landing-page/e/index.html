We lost our cap table paperwork.

We raised our seed on SAFEs from 28 investors. Some signed in one tool. Some sent their own paperwork. Some signed by email.

When we needed the documents, the data was gone. We're now digging through old inboxes and asking investors to sign again.

A cap table is the most important record a company has. It shouldn't depend on a vendor, a subscription or a login. So we built one that lives in a folder you own, and open-sourced it so it's always yours.

Install Capfolder.

Add it to your AI, ask for your cap table, and approve each change. Two minutes.

  1. 1

    Add Capfolder to your AI

    In Claude Code, add the marketplace:

    ›/plugin marketplace add MKBSP/capfolder

    Then install Capfolder from /plugin and ask: “Set up my cap table.”

    Open the Capfolder app and copy your connector link from Settings → AI. In ChatGPT, go to Settings → Apps → Create and paste it.

    Then ask ChatGPT: “Set up my cap table.” Your folder stays on your computer.

    Add the Capfolder extension to Gemini CLI:

    $gemini extensions install https://github.com/MKBSP/capfolder

    Then ask Gemini: “Set up my cap table.”

    Add Capfolder to Codex:

    $codex mcp add capfolder -- npx -y @capfolder/mcp

    Then ask Codex: “Set up my cap table.”

    Run it in the folder for your company, then follow the prompts:

    $npx capfolder install

    It sets up Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Gemini CLI, whichever you use.

    Add the Capfolder skill to any agent:

    $npx skills add MKBSP/capfolder

    Prefer clicking? Get the desktop app, with Ask AI built in.

Questions

Which AI can I use?

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude Code, or a model on your computer with Ollama or LM Studio. You bring it, so there's no AI limit from us.

Can the AI mess up my cap table?

No. It can only propose changes in the Open Cap Table Format, and nothing is saved until you approve it. Every change is logged.

Does my data go to an AI company?

Only what your AI reads to answer you, and only if you use a cloud AI. Use a model on your computer and nothing leaves it.

Is this legal advice?

No. Capfolder fills in YC's standard SAFE word for word. Have a lawyer review your documents.
