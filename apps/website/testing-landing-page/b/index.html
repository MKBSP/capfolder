Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Your cap table, people and documents have no limit. Signing through our relay is free for 5 requests a month. Pro, when it comes, adds sync and backup.

Is this legal advice?

No. Capfolder records what you and your lawyer decided. It fills in YC's standard SAFE and side letter word for word, and drafts nothing else. Have a lawyer review them.

Where is my data?

In a folder on your computer. A document you send for signature sits on the relay until it's signed, and whoever runs the relay could read it. The app sends an anonymous install count; turn it off in Settings.

Can my investor sign without installing anything?

Yes. They get an email with a link and sign in any browser. No account.

Do I need a server?

No setup needed. Download it, open it, pick a folder. Signing goes through our hosted relay; running your own is optional.

Mac, Windows or Linux?

All three. The builds aren't code-signed yet, so your computer asks you to confirm once.

Privacy

This page. Fonts, images and video come from this site. Visits, where they came from, and clicks on buttons are counted with Umami: no cookies, no personal data, your IP isn't stored. The download links are read from GitHub's public API when the page loads, so GitHub sees that request.

The download form. When you click "Download Capfolder", the form is loaded from Tally. It asks for your email and your computer, only to send you new versions. Email privacy@capfolder.com to be removed, or reply to any email.

The Pro list. "I want Pro" opens a second Tally form: your email, and optionally your company and what you'd want from Pro. We use it only to tell you when Pro is ready and give you the 25% discount.

The app. Your cap table and documents stay in your folder. Documents you send for signature pass through the signing relay until everyone has signed, then the relay deletes them (within 7 days, or 30 if never signed). The app sends an anonymous install ping (a random install id, the app version, your operating system, and yes/no for "imported", "has documents", "sent for signature") once on first run and then weekly, only after you accept the terms. The relay keeps the day, not the time or your IP address. Turn it off in Settings → Privacy. The code that sends and stores it is public in the repo.

Questions about your data. Write to privacy@capfolder.com.
