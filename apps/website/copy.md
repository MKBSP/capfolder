## From first idea to first raise.
⚠ Start the day you agree to build it together. Capfolder keeps up as the company becomes real.

1. **Idea** — Write down who's in and what each cofounder was promised. No company needed yet.
2. **Split** — Set each founder's shares and vesting. Four years with a one-year cliff, with the math shown.
3. **Incorporate** — Your promises become real Common shares, after a preview and your yes. The 83(b) deadline is flagged.
4. **Raise** — Fill the YC SAFE from a short form. Investors sign from a link. Every paper lands in your folder.

## Questions

**We haven't incorporated or raised yet. Is it for us?**
Yes. Record the split you agreed with your cofounders now. When you incorporate, it becomes real shares. When you raise, the SAFE is one form away.

**Do I need a server or any setup?**
No setup needed. Capfolder is a normal app: download it, open it, pick a folder. No server, no database, no account, no terminal. Signing goes through our hosted relay, so that needs no setup either. Running your own relay is optional.

**Is it really free?**
Yes, the whole app, with 5 signature requests a month. Pro, when it ships, adds sync and backup.

**Who can see my data?**
Your cap table and documents stay on your computer. A document you send for signature sits on the relay until it's signed (self-host the relay if that matters). The app sends an anonymous install count; turn it off in Settings.

**Do I need to pay for AI?**
No. Use a free model on your computer (Ollama or LM Studio). Your own Claude, OpenAI or Gemini key works too.

**Which companies does it support?**
Delaware C-corps first. More jurisdictions after.

**Is this legal advice?**
No. Capfolder records what you and your lawyer decided. It fills in YC's standard SAFE word for word and drafts nothing else. Have a lawyer review it.

## Privacy
⚠ (Feedback: "the privacy sections are also full of tells".)

**This page.** Fonts and images come from this site. Visits, where they came from, and clicks on buttons are counted with Umami: no cookies, no personal data, your IP isn't stored. The star count and download links are read from GitHub's public API when the page loads, so GitHub sees that request.

**The forms.** The download, Pro list and Book-a-call forms are ours. What you type is stored on Cloudflare (D1) and copied to a Google Sheet we read; your IP is hashed, not stored. A Cloudflare Turnstile check runs when you open a form, to keep bots out. After the download form we send you one email with the links and install steps, through Resend. The download form asks for your email and your computer, only to send you new versions. Email privacy@capfolder.com to be removed, or reply to any email.

**The Pro list.** "Tell me when it's ready" asks for your email, and optionally your company and what you'd want from Pro. We use it only to tell you when Pro is ready and give you the 25% discount.

**Questions about your data.** Write to privacy@capfolder.com.

**The app.** Your cap table and documents stay in your folder. Documents you send for signature pass through the signing relay until everyone has signed, then the relay deletes them (within 7 days, or 30 if never signed). The app sends an anonymous install ping (a random install id, the app version, your operating system, and yes/no for "imported", "has documents", "sent for signature") once on first run and then weekly, only after you accept the terms. The relay keeps the day, not the time or your IP address. Turn it off in Settings → Privacy. The code that sends and stores it is public in the repo.
