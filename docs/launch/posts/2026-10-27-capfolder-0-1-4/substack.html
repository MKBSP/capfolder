See who owns what, and when

Capfolder 0.1.4: a Vesting screen, a clearer person page, and an import check against Pulley, Carta or Cake

A cap table tells you who owns what today. The question people actually ask is about time: how much has vested, when is the next vest, when is it all mine? Until now, Capfolder answered that one holding at a time.

The new release puts the answer on one screen.

A Vesting screen. The option pool at a glance: total, granted, vested and still available. A row per grant opens to its graph: the cliff, the monthly steps, today, and where vesting stops if someone leaves. Key dates and signed documents sit next to it.

A person page you can read in five seconds. Vested, next vest and fully vested sit next to the name, with the graph beside the holding and its history.

An import check. Coming from Pulley, Carta or Cake? Type in the old tool's totals and Capfolder checks them to the share. A grant with more vested than granted blocks the import.

"What needs my attention?" Up to 3 things to do next, each with a Take me there button. It works with no AI connected.

Also: the Excel export opens on an Overview tab with real charts, "As of" now follows the calendar if you leave the app open overnight, and free signing is 5 requests a month.

What it doesn't do yet: the import check compares totals, not every row, so you still check a few people by hand.

Capfolder is free, open source, and keeps your cap table in a folder on your own computer. The full post, with every screenshot.
