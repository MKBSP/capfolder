How much of your equity has vested? It's a simple question. Answering it usually means opening a spreadsheet and doing some math by hand. So Capfolder 0.1.4 puts it on one screen: 1. A Vesting screen. The option pool at a glance: total, granted, vested, still available. A row per grant. Open one and you see its graph: the cliff, the monthly steps, today, and where vesting stops if someone leaves. 2. A person page you can read in five seconds. Vested, next vest and fully vested, right next to the name. 3. An import check. Moving from Pulley, Carta or Cake? Type in the old tool's totals and Capfolder checks them to the share. A grant with more vested than granted blocks the import. 4. "What needs my attention?" Up to 3 things to do next, each with a Take me there button. It works with no AI connected. One honest limit: the import check compares totals, not every row, so you still check a few people by hand. Capfolder is free, open source, and keeps your cap table in a folder on your own computer. Founders: when someone on your team asks how much of theirs has vested, how do you answer today?
