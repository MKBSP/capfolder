Would you type your cap table into an app you met today? Most cap table tools ask exactly that on day one. Share counts. Every founder. Every SAFE. All before you've seen a single useful screen. So Capfolder 0.1.3 lets you look first: 1. A sample company. Acme Inc. has 2 founders, 2 employees with options, an option pool and 3 SAFEs. Click anything. Nothing you do is saved. 2. Three yes/no questions. SAFEs? Option pool? Priced round? The steps that don't apply disappear. 3. A Get started list of 7 steps that ticks itself from what's in your cap table, so it never drifts out of date. 4. A 6-stop tour, and a "?" next to valuation cap, discount, cliff, par value and vesting start. The sample even has 11 gaps in "What's missing" on purpose: the signed documents a lawyer would ask for. You see that list on fake data before it's about your company. One honest limit: the sample is read-only, so you can't try e-signing in it. Capfolder is free, open source, and keeps your cap table in a folder on your own computer. Founders: when you first set up your cap table, what took the longest?
