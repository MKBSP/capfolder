ACME INC.
RESTRICTED STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT
This Restricted Stock Purchase Agreement is entered into as of September 14, 2021 between Acme Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and Mads Petersen (the "Purchaser").

2. 83(b) Election. The Purchaser understands that an election under Section 83(b) of the Internal Revenue Code must be filed within 30 days.
