ACME INC.
ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
The undersigned, being all of the directors of Acme Inc., a Delaware corporation, adopt the following resolutions by written consent:
Adoption of Plan. RESOLVED, that the 2025 Equity Incentive Plan, reserving 1,000,000 shares of Common Stock, is adopted and approved.
Option Grants. RESOLVED, that the Company grants an option to purchase 40,000 shares of Common Stock to Steve Wozniak at an exercise price of $0.12 per share.
Dated: September 30, 2025
